# Stage 1I — finite kari present expansion

## Goal

Extend the already-reviewed Conjugation-2A `kari` development profile without using frozen v10 answers and without opening generation to arbitrary i-final verbs.

## Independent development evidence

Primary new source family:

- Ahmed Mohamed (2013), *An Overview of the Interface between Aspects of Somali Phonology and Morphology: A Resource Guide for ESL Teachers*, St. Cloud State University, thesis p. 90 (PDF p. 102).
- The table explicitly labels `kari` as C2A and prints the clean present cells `kariyaa` (1sg/3sg masculine), `karisaan` (2pl), and `kariyaan` (3pl).

Corroboration:

- Green & Jones (2016), *A first look at the morphophonology of Marka (Af-Ashraaf) and a comparison to its neighbors*, table 24, independently prints the Northern/Benaadir Somali weak-causative present forms `kariyaa`, `karisaa`, `karinnaa`, `karisaan`, `kariyaan`.

## Conservative exclusion

The Mohamed source rendering available during review gives the 1pl cell as `kainnaa`. Because that cell is not clean enough to authorize mechanically, Stage 1I deliberately does **not** add `karinnaa`, even though other sources may attest it. The 1pl cell stays ungenerated until it is reviewed from a cleaner independent development source.

## Runtime scope

Newly authorized `kari` present cells:

- 1sg → `kariyaa`
- 3sg masculine → `kariyaa`
- 2pl → `karisaan`
- 3pl → `kariyaan`

Previously authorized and retained:

- 2sg → `karisaa`
- 3sg feminine → `karisaa`

Still unauthorized:

- 1pl
- all `kari` past cells
- any unseen i-final lemma

## Safety

- generation stays finite and lemma/person/tense specific
- no reverse suffix stripping
- no correction authority
- `nadiifi` and `qurxi` from frozen v10 remain evaluation-only and unrecognized by this rule layer
- v5–v10 development-profile disjointness is tested
